# Examples
These are fictional response fragments. They illustrate format and boundaries; they are not evidence of successful model performance. Source keys resolve in [source register](source-register.md). In an actual reply, reproduce the records for sources used and state the actual read scope.
## E1 Refine an underspecified brief
Researcher I1: “We want to survey people about why they abandon setup.”
Skill: “What decision or learning objective does this support? Which people and period do you mean? What do existing logs or research already show?”
Researcher I2: “Choose an onboarding fix. We mean last month's signups who never completed setup. Logs identify where they stopped. We don't have explanations or answer categories. We can email signups, but response yield is unknown.”
Example response:
“Provisional route: `MIXED_METHOD`. Explore recent abandonment experiences before estimating the prevalence of predefined explanations. This proposed sequence applies the distinction between defining elements and estimating their magnitude to the missing categories in your brief. [S4, ‘Moving beyond … as definitional’; I2, categories; case-specific synthesis.]
“Refined brief: choose an onboarding fix; population is last month's non-completing signups; unit is a person; logs locate abandonment. Category definitions, recruitment yield, and any later precision target remain unresolved. [I2.]
“Reported reasons can inform hypotheses. A claim that a fix causes higher completion needs a suitable causal design. [S2 §2.6.1; S4, causal-explanation section; I2, decision; synthesis.]”
The source map must include S2 and S4 with their verified locators and read status, plus I2's message locator. If only the bundled summaries were read, disclose that limitation.
## E2 A recruitment condition
Fictional I1: reviewed usable-sample target 400; 5,000 allowed unique invitations; usable-completion yield sensitivity range 0.04 to 0.08. Assume the construct is answerable as A1; this would need evidence in a real study.
Example response:
“`PLAN_WITH_CONDITIONS`. Expected usable completes range from `5,000 × 0.04 = 200` to `5,000 × 0.08 = 400`. The viability ratio is `200/400 = 0.5` to `400/400 = 1.0`. [Inputs I1; S6, formula section, algebraically expressed as expected/required completes.]
“The favorable scenario only matches the target in expectation. Request a review of achievable precision before treating the original requirement as attainable. This is a conservative planning judgment, not a probability of recruitment success. [I1; A1; S6 formula; R5; synthesis.]”
Resolve S6 in the reply and describe I1 as fictional scenario data, not observed recruitment performance.
## E3 Missing source access
Researcher I1: “Use this unfamiliar scale as a predictor of retention. The article is unavailable, but I heard it is validated.”
Example response:
“That predictive recommendation remains provisional. The brief does not provide the validation evidence or its target population and outcome. Please supply the relevant passage or authorize retrieval of an accessible source. [I1; R3 and S8, leading-indicator synopsis; original passage not inspected in this run.]”
Do not turn the scale's reputation or a citation in an unread article into a validation claim. This implements the package's citation contract.
